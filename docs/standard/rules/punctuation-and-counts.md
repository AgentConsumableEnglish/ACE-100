# Section 8 — Punctuation and counts

**ACE 8.1 (M)** — Do not use semicolons. Write two sentences.

**ACE 8.2** — Use hyphens to connect words that are directly related:

- Multi-word adjectives before a noun ("read-only replica").
- A letter or a number with a noun ("3-node cluster").
- A prefix that ends in a vowel, before a vowel ("re-index", "pre-approve").

**ACE 8.3** — Parentheses are permitted for:

- References to documents and to items.
- Declared abbreviations.
- Explanations and alternatives.

**ACE 8.4 (M)** — Do not use Latin abbreviations. Do not write "e.g.", "i.e.", or "etc.". Write "for example" or "that is", or remove them.

**ACE 8.5** — Count these as one word:

- A number, or a number with its unit.
- A backticked span.
- A declared abbreviation or acronym.
- An identifier ("web-1").
- Quoted text.
- A title or a heading.
- A proper noun of a person, a team, or an organization.
- A hyphenated word group.

**ACE 8.6** — In a vertical list, the colon of the lead-in has the function of a period. The lead-in and each item are different sentences. The applicable word limit applies to each of them.

**ACE 8.7** — Text in parentheses counts once, as one word, in the sentence that contains it. Issue 1 also counted it as its own sentence. That made the counts different for each reader, and Issue 2 repeals it.

**ACE 8.8** — This is the whole counting rule. Two readers or two tools that apply ACE 8.5 to 8.7 must get the same count.
